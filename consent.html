🎙 Voice for Muse — what it does with your data

Before first use, here's exactly what happens when you talk to Muse:

  • Your voice audio. Captured only while the mic is listening — you start it with the mic button or Alt+M, and it stops on its own after each phrase. The audio is transcribed by your browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome/Edge that means it's sent to Google's speech servers for transcription (the same service behind voice search); in Safari it's Apple's servers. The extension itself stores no audio, makes no network requests of its own, and has no servers.
  • Muse's replies. The extension reads the reply text shown on the page so it can read it aloud to you. That text is never stored or sent anywhere by the extension.
  • Your settings. Voice choice, rate, pitch, and toggles are saved only in your browser's local storage. Nothing leaves your device.

No analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
